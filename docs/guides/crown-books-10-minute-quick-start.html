Step 1 of 6 · SetupCrown Books quick start
01

Set up the company.

This happens once. It creates the company file and the owner account. You can change the company details later in Admin → Company.

  1. Business name. The name printed on invoices.
  2. Owner name and phone. The phone is optional.
  3. Owner email. This becomes your sign-in name.
  4. Password. At least 8 characters. There is no email reset in this edition.
  5. State and GSTIN. State is two digits (27 for Maharashtra); GSTIN is 15 characters.
  6. Books begin. The first day this ledger records transactions, not the financial year.
  7. Click Create company and sign in.

Migrating? Use the day after the last entry in the old system. Opening balances are as at the day before.

Step 2 of 6 · PartiesCrown Books quick start
02

Add a customer and a supplier.

One record per business. The same business can be both. The role is chosen once, when the party is created.

  1. Open Parties → All Parties and click New party.
  2. Enter Name, Phone and Email.
  3. Enter GSTIN and the two-digit State code.
  4. Set Credit days, usually 30. Credit limit is optional.
  5. Leave Opening balance at zero, or enter the old balance with the correct Dr / Cr side.
  6. Tick Customer, Supplier, or both. Click Add party.
Why the state code matters The customer’s state decides CGST + SGST or IGST on a sale. A Karnataka customer uses 29; a Maharashtra customer uses 27. The role cannot be changed after saving.

Step 3 of 6 · ItemsCrown Books quick start
03

Add an item.

An item is what you buy or sell. A goods item holds stock; a service item does not.

  1. Open Items → Items and click New item.
  2. Enter Name and SKU.
  3. Enter HSN / SAC and choose a Unit.
  4. Choose the GST rate: 0, 5, 12, 18 or 28 percent.
  5. Tick Service item (no stock) only for services.
  6. Enter Sale price and Purchase price. Click Add item.
Opening stock or purchase history, not both If you enter opening quantity for goods, do not also enter old purchase bills for the same goods. The stock and cost would be counted twice. For this first run, leave opening quantity at zero.

Step 4 of 6 · BuyingCrown Books quick start
04

Post a purchase bill.

This records what you bought and what you owe the supplier. It does not pay the supplier.

  1. Open Books → Purchases and click New bill.
  2. Choose the supplier.
  3. Check the Date and Due date.
  4. Enter the supplier’s bill number in Reference.
  5. Place of supply is the supplier’s state.
  6. Add line, choose the item, then enter Qty, Rate, Disc % and GST %.
  7. Check the Total against the paper bill.
  8. Click Post bill.
The bill is not the payment Posting the bill creates the supplier payable and brings stock in. When money leaves, record a payment separately. Full guide: section 5.5.

Step 5 of 6 · SellingCrown Books quick start
05

Post a sales invoice.

This records what the customer owes you. Every sale is against a customer; a cash sale is an invoice followed by a receipt.

  1. Open Books → Sales and click New invoice.
  2. Choose the customer.
  3. Check the Date and Due date.
  4. Enter the customer’s purchase order in Reference.
  5. Place of supply is the customer’s state.
  6. Add line, choose the item, then enter Qty and Rate.
  7. Check the tax split and Total.
  8. Click Post invoice.
The most common GST mistake The place of supply defaults to your company’s state. Change it to the customer’s state before posting an out-of-state sale, or the invoice will charge CGST + SGST instead of IGST.

Step 6 of 6 · FinishCrown Books quick start
06

Print it, prove it, back it up.

Three checks. If the paper looks right and the trial balance balances, the first cycle is complete.

01

Print

Open the sale, click Print, and check the customer, GSTIN, lines, tax and total.

02

Prove

Open Reports → Trial Balance. The green line must say “Debits equal credits.”

03

Protect

Open Admin → Backup and click Create backup before you stop.

Trial balancedebits = credits
Debits equal credits.

The trial balance ends with this line. If it is missing, stop and ask your accountant.

If something is wrong Cancel the voucher with a reason and enter it again. Posted vouchers are never edited, and the cancelled number is kept in the day book.
