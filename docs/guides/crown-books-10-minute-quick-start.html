From empty books to your first invoice

Do these six things in order. You will create the company, add one customer, add one item, post one purchase and one sale, then check that the books balance. Stop after step 6; everything else can wait.

Have this ready: business name and GSTIN, owner name and email, a password of at least 8 characters, one customer, one supplier, one item with its HSN and GST rate, and the date your books should begin.
1

Set up the company

First launch · “First run setup”
Field What to enter
Business name The name printed on invoices.
Owner name, phone The first user; the phone is optional.
Owner email Your sign-in name.
Password At least 8 characters. There is no email reset in this edition.
State, GSTIN State code is two digits (27 for Maharashtra). GSTIN is 15 characters.
Books begin The first day this app records transactions, not the financial year. Starting fresh? Use 1 April or today.

Click Create company and sign in. You can change the company details later in Admin → Company.

The one rule Nothing is saved until you press Post. Closing the screen or pressing Cancel discards the entry.
3

Add one customer and one supplier

Parties → All Parties → New party

Enter Name, Phone, GSTIN and State code. Set Credit days (30 is common). Leave Opening balance at zero for a new business. Tick Customer, Supplier, or both. Click Add party.

The role cannot be changed after saving. A customer with a Karnataka GSTIN uses state 29; a Maharashtra customer uses 27. This decides CGST + SGST or IGST on the sale.

4

Add one item

Items → Items → New item

Enter Name, SKU, HSN / SAC, choose a Unit and GST rate. Enter the Sale price and Purchase price. Tick Service item (no stock) only for a service. Leave opening quantity at zero for now. Click Add item.

5. Post a purchase bill

Books → Purchases → New bill
A purchase bill with two lines. The total is calculated before posting.
  1. Choose the supplier.
  2. Check the Date and Due date.
  3. Enter the supplier’s bill number in Reference.
  4. Place of supply is the supplier’s state.
  5. Add line, choose the item, then enter Qty, Rate, Disc % and GST %.
  6. Check the Total against the paper bill.
  7. Click Post bill.
Tip Posting the bill does not pay it. It records what you owe the supplier.

6. Post a sales invoice

Books → Sales → New invoice
A sales invoice for a Maharashtra customer, so the tax is CGST + SGST.
  1. Choose the customer.
  2. Check the Date and Due date.
  3. Enter the customer’s purchase order in Reference.
  4. Place of supply is the customer’s state. Different state means IGST.
  5. Add line, choose the item, then enter Qty and Rate.
  6. Check the tax split and Total.
  7. Click Post invoice.
  8. Open the invoice and click Print, or choose Save as PDF.
The most common GST mistake The place of supply defaults to your company’s state. Change it to the customer’s state before posting an out-of-state sale.

Finish in 60 seconds

  • Open Reports → Trial Balance. It must say “Debits equal credits.”
  • Open the sale and click Print. If the paper looks right, the invoice is right.
  • Open Admin → Backup and click Create backup. Do this before you stop for the day.

If something looks wrong

  • Wrong party, date, item or amount? Cancel the voucher with a reason and enter it again. Never edit a posted voucher.
  • Wrong GST? Check the place of supply and the GST rate on the line.
  • Stock looks double? Do not enter opening stock and purchase history for the same goods.
