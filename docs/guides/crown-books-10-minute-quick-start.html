Step 1 of 6 · SetupFirst launch → Set up the books
01

Set up the company.

This happens once. It creates the company file and the owner account. You can change the company details later in Admin → Company.

  1. Business name, owner name and phone. The phone is optional.
  2. Owner email. This becomes your sign-in name.
  3. Password. At least 8 characters. There is no email reset in this edition.
  4. State and GSTIN. State is two digits (27 for Maharashtra); GSTIN is 15 characters.
  5. Books begin. The first day this ledger records transactions, not the financial year. Migrating? Use the day after the old system’s last entry.
  6. Click Create company and sign in. Accept the numbering defaults, then follow or skip the guided tour.

Step 2 of 6 · PartiesParties → All Parties → New party
02

Add a customer and a supplier.

One record per business. The same business can be both. The role can be changed later by editing the party.

1 2

1 In the sidebar, click Parties, then All Parties. 2 Click New party. Then fill the form below.

  1. Enter Name, Phone and Email.
  2. Enter GSTIN and the two-digit State code. The state code decides CGST + SGST or IGST.
  3. Set Credit days, usually 30. Credit limit is optional.
  4. Leave Opening balance at zero for a new business, or enter the old balance with the correct Dr / Cr side.
  5. Tick Customer, Supplier, or both. You can change this later.
  6. Click Add party.

Step 3 of 6 · ItemsItems → Items → New item
03

Add an item.

An item is what you buy or sell. A goods item holds stock; a service item does not.

1 2

1 In the sidebar, click Items, then Items. 2 Click New item. Then fill the form below.

  1. Enter Name, SKU and HSN / SAC.
  2. Choose a Unit and the GST rate.
  3. Tick Service item (no stock) only for services.
  4. Enter Sale price and Purchase price.
  5. Enter Opening qty and Opening rate, or leave both at zero if you will enter old purchase bills.
  6. Click Add item.

Step 4 of 6 · BuyingPurchases → New bill
04

Post a purchase bill.

This records what you bought and what you owe the supplier. It does not pay the supplier.

1 2

1 In the sidebar, click Purchases. 2 Click New bill. Then fill the form below.

  1. Choose the supplier.
  2. Check the Date and Due date. Enter the supplier’s bill number in Reference; set Place of supply to the supplier’s state.
  3. Click Add line and choose the item.
  4. Enter Qty, Rate, Disc % and GST %.
  5. Check the Total against the paper bill.
  6. Click Post bill. This records the payable; it does not pay the supplier.

Step 5 of 6 · SellingSales → New invoice
05

Post a sales invoice.

This records what the customer owes you. Every sale is against a customer; a cash sale is an invoice followed by a receipt.

1 2

1 In the sidebar, click Sales. 2 Click New invoice. Then fill the form below.

  1. Choose the customer.
  2. Check the Date and Due date. Enter the customer’s purchase order in Reference.
  3. Set Place of supply to the customer’s state. Same state = CGST + SGST; different state = IGST. The default is your company’s state, so change it for an out-of-state customer.
  4. Click Add line, choose the item, then enter Qty and Rate.
  5. Check the tax split and Total.
  6. Click Post invoice.

Step 6 of 6 · FinishSales → Invoice → Print
06

Print it, prove it, back it up.

Three checks. If the paper looks right and the trial balance balances, the first cycle is complete.

01

Print

Open the sale, click Print, and check the customer, GSTIN, lines, tax and total.

02

Prove

Open Reports → Trial Balance. The green line must say “Debits equal credits.”

03

Protect

Open Admin → Backup and click Create backup before you stop.

Trial balancedebits = credits
Debits equal credits.

The trial balance ends with this line. If it is missing, stop and ask your accountant.

If something is wrong Cancel the voucher with a reason and enter it again. Posted vouchers are never edited, and the cancelled number is kept in the day book.
