Your books, one step at a time

Crown Books · 1.0

Crown Books keeps the accounts of one business on this computer. It answers three questions: who owes us, who do we owe, and do the books balance. This guide shows the actual screens and the buttons to press.

01

Who owes us?

Sales invoices, receipts, receivables and ageing.

02

Who do we owe?

Purchase bills, payments, payables and due dates.

03

Do the books balance?

Trial balance, profit and loss, and the balance sheet.

Before you start

  • Business name, address, state and GSTIN.
  • Owner name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  • The date your books should begin.
  • Customer and supplier list, with phone and GSTIN where known.
  • Item list with HSN or SAC, unit, GST rate, sale and purchase price.
  • Opening stock, party balances and cash or bank balances.

How to read a step

Button and field names appear in bold exactly as they appear on screen. Numbers and voucher numbers appear in monospace.

A red number on a screenshot matches a numbered step or key beside it. Reference figures use the key printed under the picture.

Tip Do one chapter at a time. The first five steps in chapter 1 are enough to post your first invoice. Everything else can wait until you need it.
The one rule that prevents most mistakes Nothing is saved until you press Post. Closing the screen or pressing Cancel discards the entry. A posted voucher is never edited: cancel it with a reason and enter a new one.
