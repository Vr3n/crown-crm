Company and opening balances

The company profile is printed on every document. The chart of accounts is the list of ledgers behind the reports. Opening balances are the one-time bridge from your old books.

In this chapterCompany settings · Books begin · Chart of accounts · Opening balances

2.1Company settings

Open Admin → Company. The page has four sections: identity, financial year, stock and rounding, and invoice terms. The first two are printed on documents; the last two control costing and rounding.

Registered name
The name printed on invoices, usually the legal or trading name.
GSTIN, PAN, state code
Registration details. The state code is two digits, for example 27 for Maharashtra.
Address, city, pincode
The address printed above the invoice lines.
Financial year starts
The month your reporting year starts. April is the default for India.
Books begin date
The first day this app records transactions. See 2.2.
Costing method
Keep Weighted average. This edition values stock at weighted average cost.
Round off
How the invoice total is rounded. Invoice total is the usual choice.
Default terms
Saved with the company, but not printed by the current invoice layout.
Leave this unticked Allow selling below stock on hand is recorded as a setting but the current build does not enforce a stock guard. Keep it unticked and check the stock summary before a large sale.

2.2What “books begin” means

Books begin is the first day Crown Books is responsible for the ledger. Before that date, the history lives in your old system, your accountant’s file, or nowhere. At that date you bring in one opening position: what you own, what you owe, and what customers owe you.

It is not the financial year. A business can have a financial year from 1 April to 31 March and still begin its Crown Books ledger on 1 October after migrating from Tally. An October invoice still belongs to the current April-to-March year.

Situation What to enter
Starting a new business The day you want the books to start, often 1 April or the day of the first sale.
Fresh financial year 1 April of that year. Opening balances are the previous 31 March closing balances.
Migrating mid-year The day after the last entry in the old system. Opening balances are as at the close of the day before.
Only using Crown Books for sales Still use the real start date. Do not enter a later date just to avoid old data.
Opening balances are the day before If the old system is closed on 30 September and books begin is 1 October, the opening balances are the position at the close of 30 September. Post the opening voucher dated 1 October, then start entering October transactions.

2.3Chart of accounts

Every voucher writes to ledgers. The chart of accounts is the list of those ledgers, grouped into assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Crown Books seeds a working set so you do not have to create one from scratch.

The four cards split the ledger into Assets, Liabilities, Income and Expenses. The Balance by group chart ranks the groups by value, so a large balance in an unexpected group is visible before you read the rows. It reads the same accounts as the table below.

  1. Click New account.
  2. Enter a Name, for example Office Expenses.
  3. Choose a Group. It decides the nature, for example Indirect Expenses.
  4. Check the Type. It follows the group but can be adjusted.
  5. Leave the opening balance at zero unless you are setting up the opening position.
  6. Click Add account.

Reserved ledgers have a fixed job: sales, purchases, GST, inventory, cost of goods sold, round off and the opening difference. Add your own ledgers for rent, salaries or travel without touching them.

The chart is gated by the company.view permission. A user without it does not see Masters in the rail, and the page cannot be opened by typing its address.

2.4Opening balances

Opening balances come from three places: the party record, the account record and the item record. They are collected into one opening voucher so that the trial balance stays balanced.

  1. Add party opening balances in the party records. Positive means the party owes you; negative means you owe the party.
  2. Add the opening balance and side on each bank, cash, loan or capital account.
  3. Add opening quantity and rate on each goods item. Services do not hold stock.
  4. Open Admin → Company, scroll to Opening balances, and choose the Opening date.
  5. Click Post opening balances. One OPN/0001 voucher is created.
  6. Open the trial balance and check that debits equal credits.

2.5Opening balance checklist

  • The old system is closed and reconciled on the day before books begin.
  • Every customer and supplier balance has the correct Dr or Cr side.
  • Cash, bank, loans and capital are entered on the account records.
  • Goods have opening quantity and rate, or zero if you are bringing purchase history.
  • The opening date matches the day before the first new transaction.
  • The trial balance says “Debits equal credits” and Opening Balance Difference is zero or explained.

After the opening voucher is posted, the opening fields are locked. Corrections go through a journal, not by editing the voucher.
