P0

First run and the shell

A new machine has no company. Setup captures the business name, owner, state, GSTIN and books begin date, then creates the company, five roles, nineteen account groups, the reserved accounts, units, tax rates and payment methods in one transaction.

P1

Parties, items, accounts and company

One row per business with customer and supplier flags. Creating a customer or supplier also creates its ledger account under Sundry Debtors or Sundry Creditors. Items carry HSN, unit, GST and opening stock. The company screen holds the financial year, costing method and rounding policy.

Test asserts the created customer and supplier appear in the list and the item appears in the item table before moving on.
P2 · P4

Purchase bill, payment and the balance proof

The purchase screen is a document, not a form. Ten units at 3,000 with 18 percent GST gives 30,000 taxable, 2,700 CGST, 2,700 SGST and 35,400 payable. Posting writes the voucher, item lines, ledger lines and ten stock movements in one transaction. A 10,000 payment is then applied against the bill.

This is the strongest proof in the suite: a document enters once, and both the payable and the trial balance agree without a reconciliation step.
P3

Sales invoice, receipt with allocation, receivables and ageing

The same engine runs the sale. One SSD at 3,855.93 plus 18 percent gives 4,550.00, with one paisa of round off. A 2,000 receipt is split 1,500 against the invoice and 500 left unapplied. Receivables then show 3,050.00 and the ageing report buckets the open bill.

P5 · P6

Stock and the financial statements

Stock on hand is the sum of movements, never a stored number. The purchase added ten units and the sale removed one. The registers carry the GST split. Profit and loss nets sales against cost of goods sold, and the balance sheet proves assets equal liabilities plus equity.

The balance sheet check is computed from the same ledger rows as the trial balance, so the two cannot disagree.
P2 · P8

Contra and local backup

A contra moves cash into the bank through the same posting engine. Backup checkpoints the WAL and copies the database file, so the copy is consistent without stopping the app.

PERSISTENCE

Close the app, reopen it, the books are still there

The test closes Electron, relaunches it against the same database file, and checks the dashboard and receivables again. The remembered session restores, and the 3,050.00 is computed from rows, not from a cache.
