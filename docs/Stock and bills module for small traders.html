Stock on hand is a sum of movements, so you never edit a quantity

+10 buy

Stock on hand = 10 − 3 − 1 = 6. A count of the shelf makes an adjustment movement. It does not overwrite 6.

Allow negative stock with a warning. Small shops often sell before they enter the purchase.
